CARDIAC PRE-STROKE

AI-based ECG Prediction System

By: Kareem Ismail & Zeyad Ayman

An advanced AI-driven cardiac system that predicts early stroke and heart attack risks using high-resolution ECG micro-dynamic analysis.

Summary of the Idea

Cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of global deaths. This project aims to develop an AI system capable of detecting invisible micro-dynamic changes in ECG signals that can indicate early stages of coronary artery disease and potential stroke risks.

Unlike traditional ECG-based diagnostics that rely on visible waveform features, our model focuses on deep micro-level analysis through advanced signal processing and hybrid deep learning architectures (CNN + LSTM).

Procedures and Methods

1️⃣ ECG signals were collected from multiple datasets, including healthy and pre-stroke patients.

2️⃣ Signal preprocessing was performed with adaptive noise filtering to ensure clarity and quality.

3️⃣ Micro-dynamic feature extraction captured subtle variations between heartbeats (SD, max, min values).

4️⃣ A hybrid CNN-LSTM model was trained to detect early pre-stroke risk patterns, achieving up to 90% accuracy.

5️⃣ A prototype Streamlit app allows ECG upload and automatic prediction in real-time.

Results and Data Analysis

✅ The model achieved 90% accuracy compared to 82–86% in previous studies.

✅ The QRS Complex was identified as the most influential ECG component (60%) in early diagnosis.

✅ Integration of micro-dynamic analysis improved diagnostic precision and stability under noisy data conditions.

These findings show that micro-level ECG analysis is a strong predictor of pre-stroke risk and can significantly enhance early cardiac care.

Future Work

  • Integrating real-time wearable ECG monitoring devices.
  • Expanding datasets from hospitals for better generalization.
  • Adding blood flow and artery diameter estimation for higher prediction accuracy.
  • Developing a cloud-based platform for ECG uploading and instant AI-based reporting.
