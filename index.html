CARDIAC PRE-STROKE

AI-Synchronized ECG System for Early Stroke Risk Prediction in CAD Patients

Authors: Kareem Ismail – Zeyad Ayman

Summary of The Idea:

This project constitutes a fully integrated AI-driven system capable of extracting highly sensitive micro-dynamic repolarization from ECG records with personalized beat segments. By combining QT variability and rapid instability indices, the system establishes an automated framework for early cardiac-risk detection.


🚀 Try the App

⚠️ Problem Statement

Global Burden: Cardiovascular diseases are a leading cause of death globally. Early detection is critical.

Hidden Early Abnormalities: A major challenge is that high-risk changes often manifest as "micro-dynamic" variations not visible to the human eye. This means the patient may be approaching a heart attack or stroke invisible to standard clinical tools.

Clinical Limitations: Raw ECG signals often contain noise and fail to capture "spatial" indoctrination features required for accurate classification.

📜 Background of Research

Although previous research has created crucial advances in cardiac vascular disease diagnosis, they exhibit critical limitations:

  • 1. Population-dependent Issues: Most research, such as studies on QT interval variability (QTV), examines varied patients using the same databases (Al-Zaiti et al., 2020) or large general populations, without capturing the nuances of individual cardiac patterns.
  • 2. Limited Real-World Applicability: ECG biometric studies often focus on controlled lab settings or standardized datasets, which fail to account for daily-life monitoring, resulting in low-quality continuous tracking and potentially dangerous oversight of patients' status (Lehmann & Buschek, 2020).
  • 3. Traditional Focusing: These studies concentrated on detecting the presence or absence of CAD, without providing early assessment based on micro-dynamics variability.

🎯 Engineering Goals

The main functionality of this project is designing and generating an advanced AI-driven system that has more capabilities of detecting micro-dynamic variations in ECG signals than any other.

  • Advanced AI Detection: Processing ECG signals (feature, noise filtering, dynamic variations) to capture invisible micro-patterns.
  • Hybrid Deep Learning Model: Implementing a hybrid engineering model that integrates XGBoost for spatial feature indoctrination and layers for temporal dependency modeling. Goal: >91.5% prediction accuracy.
  • Real-Time Modular Framework: Software supporting real-time data acquisition, AI modeling, and quality engineering.
  • Hardware Integration: Analytic abilities with hardware scale (computational efficiency) and Arduino capabilities with hospital datasets to guarantee practical clinical deployment.

⚙️ Procedures and Methods

  • 1. Data Collection: ECG signals were collected from multiple clinical databases to ensure diverse and unbiased data.
  • 2. Data Preprocessing: Raw ECG signals are cleaned, filtered, normalized, and segmented into fixed windows.
  • 3. Micro-Dynamics Zoom: Micro-dynamics analysis reveals tiny heartbeat electrical activity of the heart before processing.
  • 4. AI Model Training: The model was trained using an 80/20 train-test split. The architecture is a Stacking Ensemble optimizing LightGBM, XGBoost, and Random Forest.
  • 5. Model Validation: Evaluated using 20% unseen test data to ensure fairness. Confusion matrix, accuracy, precision, and recall were calculated.
  • 6. Hardware Integration: The setup uses an ECG sensor connected to an Arduino board to capture real-time heart signals. The device preprocesses the signal and sends it to the mobile app for further AI analysis.
  • 7. Analysis Application: A dedicated doctor analysis application was developed to review patient ECG uploads remotely with higher accuracy.

Specificity

The model achieved a 91.5% accuracy across eight ECG classes, demonstrating high reliability in multi-class cardiac classification. Micro-dynamic features significantly boosted performance, allowing the system to detect subtle electrical instabilities invisible in standard ECG readings.

Feature Analysis: Showed that RMS, variance, skewness, kurtosis, and micro-dynamics were the most influential predictors.

Risk Stratification: The model successfully separated normal and micro-abnormal ECGs, identifying early pre-stroke risk patterns. Analysis revealed clear clustering into low, moderate, and high-risk groups.

🌍 Impact & Conclusion

Analysis Impact: This project, with assistance from creative methods based on physiological knowledge, produces a complete and validated early-risk detection capable of processing ECG records and specifically extracting biomarkers.

Final Impact: This system offers a scalable, rapid, and non-invasive approach which addresses a major clinical gap in early cardiac-risk recognition through binding proactive monitoring and earlier alarms. This supports preventive cardiology and reduces wrong alarms.
