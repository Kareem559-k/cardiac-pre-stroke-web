CARDIAC PRE-STROKE

AI-Synchronized ECG System for Early Stroke Risk Prediction in CAD Patients

Authors: Kareem Ismail – Zeyad Ayman

Cardiovascular diseases are the leading cause of death globally. The project aims to develop an AI-based model that detects micro-dynamic ECG variations to predict early risks of heart attack and stroke in coronary artery disease (CAD) patients.

🚀 Try the App

⚠️ Problem Statement

Cardiovascular diseases cause nearly one-third of global deaths every year. Despite advances in medical technology, early detection of coronary artery disease (CAD) and stroke remains challenging.

Traditional ECG analysis only detects visible waveform changes after disease progression. However, tiny electrical micro-dynamics occur much earlier — invisible to the human eye or standard tools.

Missing these subtle signals leads to delayed diagnosis and preventable cardiac events. Thus, there is an urgent need for an AI system capable of detecting micro-level ECG variations, predicting early risks, and supporting real-time monitoring through wearable or hospital-based devices.

🧠 Project Summary

This project introduces an AI-powered system designed to detect the earliest invisible ECG variations that indicate risks of heart attack and stroke. Unlike traditional models that focus on visible heart rate or rhythm changes, our system analyzes micro-dynamic patterns hidden within each heartbeat.

By combining deep learning (CNN + LSTM) with advanced ECG signal processing, the model achieves over 90% accuracy in predicting pre-stroke and coronary artery disease conditions.

This innovation represents a major step toward smart, preventive cardiac care — giving doctors the ability to detect danger before symptoms appear, potentially saving thousands of lives through early intervention.

⚙️ Methods

  • Data Collection: ECG data were gathered from accurate databases (e.g., PhysioNet) including both healthy and pre-stroke patients.
  • Preprocessing: Missing values handled via imputation; features normalized to unify signal scales.
  • Feature Extraction: Micro-dynamics analyzed — including standard deviation, maximum, and minimum variations — to represent hidden cardiac patterns.
  • Model Development: Hybrid architecture combining CNN (spatial features) and LSTM (temporal patterns); achieved ≈90% accuracy on unseen data.
  • Prototype: Streamlit-based web app allowing ECG file upload (HEA, DAT, etc.) and instant stroke risk prediction.

📊 Results

  • The QRS complex had the highest diagnostic importance (~60%), followed by P-wave and T-wave.
  • Accuracy improved from 78% to 90% after applying micro-dynamic analysis.
  • Compared with other studies (82%, 86%), this model achieved 90%, showing superior precision.
  • An inverse relation was found between blood flow and ST-segment elevation, confirming physiological behavior.

Conclusion: The model successfully detects early cardiac abnormalities, surpassing previous works in accuracy and reliability.

🚀 Future Work

  • Expand datasets from multiple hospitals for broader generalization.
  • Integrate with wearable ECG devices for continuous monitoring.
  • Conduct clinical validation with real patient data.
  • Develop a cloud platform for real-time ECG uploading and AI reporting.
  • Implement personalized AI learning adapting to each user’s heart pattern.
