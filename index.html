CARDIAC PRE-STROKE

AI-Synchronized ECG System for Early Stroke Risk Prediction in CAD Patients

Authors: Kareem Ismail – Zeyad Ayman

Summary of The Idea:

This project constitutes a fully integrated AI-driven system capable of extracting highly sensitive micro-dynamic repolarization from ECG records with personalized beat segments. By combining QT variability and rapid instability indices, the system establishes an automated framework for early cardiac-risk detection.


🚀 Try the App

Problem Statement

Global Burden

Cardiovascular diseases are a leading cause of death globally. Early detection is critical.

Hidden Abnormalities

High-risk changes often manifest as "micro-dynamic" variations not visible to the human eye or standard tools.

Clinical Limitations

Raw ECG signals often contain noise and fail to capture "spatial" indoctrination features required for accurate classification.

Background of Research

Although previous research has created crucial advances, they exhibit critical limitations:

1. Population-dependent Issues

Most research on QT variability examines varied patients using the same databases or large general populations, without capturing the nuances of individual cardiac patterns.

2. Limited Real-world Applicability

Studies often focus on controlled lab settings, failing to account for daily-life monitoring, resulting in low-quality continuous tracking.

3. Traditional Focusing

Concentrated on detecting the presence or absence of CAD, without providing early assessment based on micro-dynamics variability.

Engineering Goals

The main functionality is designing an advanced AI-driven system detecting micro-dynamic variations better than any other.

  • Advanced AI Detection System: Processing high-precision ECG signals (feature extraction, noise filtering) to capture invisible micro-patterns.
  • Hybrid Deep Learning Model: Integrating XGBoost for spatial feature indoctrination and temporal dependency modeling. Target: >91.5% accuracy.
  • Real-Time Modular Framework: Software that supports real-time data acquisition and parallel implication for continuous cardiac supervising.
  • Hardware Integration: Integrating analytic abilities with hardware scale and Arduino capabilities with hospital datasets for practical clinical deployment.

Procedures and Methods

1. Data Collection

ECG signals collected from multiple clinical databases to ensure diverse and unbiased data.

2. Data Preprocessing

Raw signals are cleaned, filtered, normalized, and segmented into fixed windows.

3. AI Model Training

Trained on 80/20 split. Stacking Ensemble optimizing LightGBM, XGBoost, Random Forest.

Hardware Integration

Uses an ECG sensor connected to an Arduino to capture real-time signals. The device preprocesses signals and sends them to the mobile app.

Analysis Application

A dedicated doctor analysis app developed to review patient ECG uploads remotely, generating micro-dynamics reports.

Conclusion and Discussion

Final Achievement

A fully integrated AI-driven system extracting highly sensitive micro-dynamic repolarization. Combining QT variability and rapid instability indices for early detection.

Results

The model demonstrated solid diagnostic performance: 92% accuracy (approx), 91% sensitivity, 93% specificity. Subtle early-risk signatures are indeed embedded within ECG.

Analysis Impact

Produces a complete and validated early-risk detection capable of specifically extracting biomarkers, suitable for wearable devices.

Impact

Offers a scalable, rapid, non-invasive approach addressing a major clinical gap through proactive monitoring and earlier alarms.
